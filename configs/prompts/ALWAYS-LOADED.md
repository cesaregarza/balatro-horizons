# Persistent instructions

- Apply visible deck, stake, boss, voucher and Joker effects over the defaults below.
- Ordinary interest pays $1 per full $5 of eligible cash, capped at $5; active effects can change this.
- Ordinary unused hands pay $1 each. Use native settlement timing for income and costs.
- Score beyond the current blind's target does not carry forward.
- Cash, surviving deck changes, Jokers and hand levels persist across rounds.
- Skipping forgoes the played round's normal settlement and shop; its tag grants its own reward.
- Look up uncertain rules. Preserve useful facts and plans in memory_update.
