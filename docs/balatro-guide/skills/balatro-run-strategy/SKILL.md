# Maintain a contingent run plan

Plan around attainable hands and visible opportunities. Assess progress against upcoming targets and Boss effects rather than a fixed Joker milestone for each Ante.

Early: obtain a scoring floor from available hands and affordable offers; take income or scaling when the next Blind remains viable. Middle: connect repeatable hand families, Chips, early Mult, multiplication, consistency and growth to the revealed Boss. Late: spend on a reliable finish when investment has too little time to repay, accounting for cash-dependent score and reserved Boss-control fees.

| Engine family | Development opportunity | Fragility to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High Card/Pair | Easy-to-form hands; independent/held effects | Insufficient base growth or dependence on one effect |
| Flush | Suit consolidation and scored-card synergies | Suit debuffs and limited future scaling |
| Straight | Planet growth and preserved rank windows | Gaps and fragile completion paths |
| Repeated rank / secret hands | Copies of strong cards | Draw density and an accidental hand-level pivot |
| Retriggers | Repeated valuable card events | Wrong first scorer, debuffs, phase confusion |
| Held-card engine | Small plays retaining support | Hand-size loss, forced cards/discards and debuffs |
| Plasma | Grow effective Chips plus Mult | Applying ordinary product-balancing advice |

These overlap. Commit based on real support and attainable draws; pivot when the transition cost is covered. Levels and money already spent are not reasons to persist with a failing plan, but an unsupported attractive card is not sufficient reason to abandon a working engine.

Use three planning horizons: **immediate = survive this Blind; short = survive this Ante; long = survive this run**. Compare a choice across these horizons when it trades present strength for development. Complete the native run; reaching a later Ante alone does not satisfy the win objective.

Keep only a compact optional memory of hand family, bottleneck, next useful acquisition class, income/growth, revealed Boss interaction and pivot conditions. It contains public facts and conditional intentions, not future outcomes. A reliable clear is valuable; growth farming can trade present resources for later survival when the remaining draw paths and Boss restrictions justify the risk.
