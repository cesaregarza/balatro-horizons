# Apply the revealed Boss to this build

Read the [Boss catalog](references/bosses.md) for effects, then evaluate how the revealed Boss interacts with the current build.

Identify the dependencies the Boss changes: hand family, faces, suit, first scoring card, held cards, discards, hand size, one Joker, order or cash. Evaluate favorable interactions too: a largely independent-Joker engine can be comparatively resilient to card debuffs, leaving an opportunity to invest.

Recompute the actual score/resources under the effect. A debuffed Flush can still outperform an off-suit fallback through hand base and independent Jokers. A playable hand can score zero under a Boss restriction. Do not estimate viability as best-hand score times remaining hands without considering draw reliability and restrictions.

Compare available counters: altered card selection, leveled fallback, consumable, sellable spare, affordable reroll or correctly timed disable. A Luchador that might appear later is an uncertain acquisition, not an available counter. Shops before the Boss have value when they can repair the interaction.

Selling Luchador before entering the Boss does not execute its in-Boss disable. Eternal can invalidate a Verdant Leaf sale plan. Amber Acorn invalidates assumed positions; only public observations or lawful continuity can restore identity. Crimson Heart's disabled Joker must be reassessed after a hand, not assumed to change on discard.

Under The Eye, reserve useful distinct categories for later hands. Under The Mouth, commit to a repeatable sufficient category. Under The Needle/Water, inspect post-selection counters for modifiers. Under The Serpent, compare the three-card refill with the score and retained-hand effects of the proposed selection.

Choose the supported counter or best available survival attempt. Optional notes can identify the dependency, timing and uncertainty.
