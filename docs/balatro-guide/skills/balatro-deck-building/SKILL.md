# Shape the deck around useful roles

Read [modifier mechanics](references/modifiers.md) for a relevant layer or trigger.

Distinguish full deck, current public remaining multiset, held cards and used cards; keep duplicate identities separate. Assess each card as a played scorer, held asset and discard asset. Decoration alone does not make it useful.

An edit should improve achievable score, reliability or resources while covering the next Blind. Copies and transformations can concentrate strong properties; removing irrelevant cards raises density. Added cards also consume future draws. There is no universal minimum deck size: compare actual expected cycling with available cards.

For Straights, preserve rank windows; removing faces or Aces indiscriminately can create gaps. For repeated ranks, strengthen the supported rank and useful modifier bundle. For Flushes, consider suit density and the revealed suit counter. Small-hand engines may prefer held Steel/Gold/Blue support to another large-hand pattern.

Account for a new higher-precedence hand routing onto a weaker level, enhancement replacement, Glass destruction, random removal, and pre-scoring effects such as Vampire removing an enhancement. A Wild card broadens suit membership and Boss exposure, not rank membership.

After each edit refresh the public counts, legal targets and scoring assumptions. Decline an off-plan Standard Pack card when dilution outweighs its value; the purchase does not obligate a pick.
