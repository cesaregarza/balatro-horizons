# Use the consumable in its actual context

Load the [catalog](references/catalog.md) for a named effect and [modifiers](../balatro-deck-building/references/modifiers.md) for enhancement/seal layers.

Read the supported phase, target count, physical positions, available temporary/round hand, capacity and effect text. “Up to two” and “exactly two” differ. For Death, verify the overwrite card is physically left of the copied card. Use only available targets; a known full-deck card is not automatically targetable.

Use a Planet before its intended scoring benefit; with Observatory compare a permanent level against the value of holding it. Concentrate levels when the hand is repeatable, and keep a useful fallback when the Boss or deck warrants one. Compare the improvement with its cost and the next Blind's needs.

Evaluate money effects at use time and generators at resolution capacity. Leave space for useful Blue/Purple generation when doing so helps. The winning hand may determine Blue Seal's Planet; it need not be the most-played hand.

Seals and transformations serve different roles: played scorer, held support or discarded generator. Compare a copied strong card with the lost rank coverage or held value. Sigil is random and can harm a suit engine; Immolate can destroy key cards; Wraith spends all cash; Ankh/Hex require checking possible survivors; Ectoplasm/Ouija cost hand size. “Spectral” and “safe upgrade” are not interchangeable.

After buying a pack, evaluate the revealed choices; its cost is sunk. Taking nothing can be correct. For multi-pick packs, apply one choice and re-observe before choosing the next. Normal pack consumables are used immediately; Standard cards join the deck and Buffoon Jokers enter inventory. Follow an observed exception when present.
