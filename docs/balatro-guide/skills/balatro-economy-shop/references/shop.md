# Shop, settlement, packs and vouchers

Use the current offers, prices, capacity and effect text when modifiers change these defaults.

## Stock and prices

A default shop has two individual offers, two packs and an Ante voucher. Rerolls replace individual offers, not packs/voucher. A new shop restocks packs; ordinary voucher stock changes with Ante. The first shop includes a normal Buffoon Pack in this rules baseline.

| Item | Imported base price |
| --- | --- |
| Tarot / Planet | $3 |
| Spectral | $4 |
| Playing card | $1 |
| Normal / Jumbo / Mega pack | $4 / $6 / $8 |
| Voucher | $10 |
| Joker | Individual card price; rarity alone does not determine it |

Typical edition surcharges are Foil +$2, Holographic +$3, Polychrome/Negative +$5. Sell value is normally half the relevant cost, floored, minimum $1, subject to Rental and value-changing effects. Use displayed buy/sell prices when discounts or other modifiers apply.

Rerolls normally start at $5 and increase by $1 in the same shop; Reroll Surplus reduces the base to $3, Reroll Glut to $1. D6 makes the next shop's starting reroll cost $0; free-reroll effects such as Chaos the Clown have their own handling. Inspect the next displayed price rather than inferring all interactions.

Owned Jokers and held consumables are normally excluded from duplicate shop offers; Showman permits duplicates. Special creation, tags and copy effects can have different eligibility.

## Interest and settlement

Ordinary interest planning formula: `min(cap, floor(max(0, eligible_cash)/5))`, baseline cap $5. Seed Money raises the cap to $10 and Money Tree to $20. Green Deck has no base interest; its unused-resource payout is $2 per hand and $1 per discard.

Interest depends on the eligible balance at settlement. The exact ordering of Gold, Rental, Joker income and cash-out awards is a [known limit](../../../KNOWN_LIMITS.md). Do not include unreceived money in an exact interest forecast unless the relevant ordering is established.

## Packs

| Family | Normal choices | Jumbo choices | Mega choices | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3, take 1 | 5, take 1 | 5, take 2 | Playing cards join deck |
| Arcana | 3, use 1 | 5, use 1 | 5, use 2 | Tarot use with temporary hand |
| Celestial | 3, use 1 | 5, use 1 | 5, use 2 | Planet use |
| Buffoon | 2, take 1 | 4, take 1 | 4, take 2 | Jokers, subject to capacity |
| Spectral | 2, use 1 | 4, use 1 | 4, use 2 | Spectral use with temporary hand |

Normal pack consumables are immediately used; generated consumables may enter slots. This does not mean all pack cards are consumed. Arcana/Spectral expose an actual temporary hand for supported targeting. Pack choice count and current legal options outrank the table. A pack can be skipped after opening. Packs from skip tags can open outside a shop.
