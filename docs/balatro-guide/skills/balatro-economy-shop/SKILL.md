# Spend for the actual bottleneck

Read [shop and vouchers](references/shop.md) for stock, prices and effects, or the [tag catalog](references/tags.md) for a skip reward.

Read visible prices, sell values, slots, cash-dependent score, liabilities, next Blind and revealed Boss. Compare visible offers against the current bottleneck: Chips, early Mult, multiplication, consistency, growth, money, capacity or a specific Boss interaction. Buy enough strength to survive, then invest when the expected opportunity remains useful.

The $25 interest target is conditional. A purchase below it can save the run or earn back income by clearing earlier. Its future interest cost lasts only while the balance remains below the relevant threshold; it is not automatically a permanent $1-per-round loss. Green Deck has no base interest but still values cash for purchases and effects.

For a sale, evaluate the remaining build, copy targets, cash-linked score and Negative capacity before assuming the replacement works. Rental costs accumulate over rounds held; Perishable value has an expiry; Eternal may make a proposed sale impossible. An economy Joker can stay useful above $25, and flat scoring can stay useful late.

Reroll with a target class and a purchase budget after the fee. A visible usable upgrade can beat a speculative ideal. Compare vouchers with remaining opportunities: hand/hand-size upgrades affect reliability; interest upgrades require cash; Boss control needs its fee; Ante reduction also removes a resource. There is no universal voucher or pack priority list.

For a skip, compare the displayed tag's timing with forgone income, shop access, triggers and scaling, and with the next Blind's safety. Price a rarity/edition tag against the shop and income forgone. Save Anaglyph Double Tags for opportunities that justify that cost. A Boss Tag can justify skipping when it directly addresses a threat.

Make one purchase/sale/reroll/skip/exit action, then re-observe before dependent choices.
