# BalatroBench: Building an LLM Benchmark for Horizon-Balancing Reasoning in Balatro

## TL;DR
- **A project named "BalatroBench" already exists** (coder/balatrobench, live at balatrobench.com, ~22 GitHub stars as of early 2026) and sits on a mature open-source stack — BalatroBot (a Steamodded mod exposing a JSON-RPC API, ~60 stars) and BalatroLLM (an LLM harness). But it measures only a shallow slice of what you want: it scores "average round reached" and tool-call reliability on the easiest configuration (Red deck, White stake), so it does **not** decompose immediate/short/long-horizon reasoning and has no defenses against training-data contamination.
- **The technical route is largely solved for you**: BalatroBot lets an LLM read structured JSON game state and take every action (play/discard/buy/sell/reroll/skip/use), with `--headless` and `--fast` flags, deterministic seed strings, and per-instance ports for parallelism. This is the fastest MVP path. High-fidelity open-source simulators (Python `balatro-gym`, `balatro_sim`; Rust `balatro-rs`) exist but are partial reimplementations that must be validated against the real game.
- **Balatro is an unusually good horizon-balancing testbed** — exponential score scaling forces immediate (which 5 cards), short-term (beat this blind, spend this shop), and long-term (build a scaling engine for ante 8+) decisions — but you must engineer around contamination (wiki, joker lists and strategy are all online) and high variance (skilled humans win ~70% of full runs; the best public RL agent tops out at 2.35%). Your differentiated contribution should be **horizon-decomposed sub-metrics + contamination-robust variants (custom jokers, renamed jokers, rule perturbations, held-out seeds)**, not another "rounds reached" leaderboard.

## Key Findings

### 1. Prior art: "BalatroBench" is already taken — but it's shallow
The name and the basic idea already exist as a coordinated three-repo project by the company **Coder** (coder.com), plus community forks and adjacent tools:

- **coder/balatrobench** — a benchmark analysis tool + leaderboard (balatrobench.com). MIT licensed. Scores models by **average final round reached** (out of 24), plus tool-call reliability (valid+executable / valid+non-executable / invalid), token usage, latency, and cost per tool call. Data is published on Kaggle. Protocol: Red deck, White stake, fixed seeds (AAAAA, BBBBB, CCCCC, DDDDD, EEEEE), 3 plays per seed = 15 runs. As of the Feb 13 2026 GIGAZINE writeup, the top model was **gemini-3-pro-preview**, which cleared round 24 nine times out of 15; the worst was mistral-large-2512 (usually lost round 1).
- **coder/balatrobot** — the workhorse: a Steamodded mod serving a **JSON-RPC 2.0 HTTP API** exposing game state and full controls. ~60 stars, 29 releases (latest v1.5.2, Jun 2026), Python+Lua, MIT. It's a fork of the original **besteon/balatrobot** (acknowledging phughesion, besteon, giewev).
- **coder/balatrollm** — the LLM bot that reads state, picks actions from Jinja2-templated strategies, and calls the API. Generates the run artifacts BalatroBench parses.
- **BalatroBench "Community" mode** — runs all strategies on a fixed model (`openai/gpt-oss-20b`, medium reasoning), Red deck, White stake, 5 fixed 7-char seeds × 3 = 15 games, to measure *strategy* quality rather than model capability.

Adjacent/older projects:
- **proj-airi/game-playing-ai-balatro** — CV+LLM (YOLO/RapidOCR) vision-based agent; trained a YOLOv11n entity detector.
- **taggarttufte/balatro-rl** — PPO RL agent + full Python simulator (`balatro_sim`, 164 jokers, 496 tests). Concluded April 2026 at a **2.35% peak win rate** (~235× random), concluding shaped-reward PPO can't discover Balatro's multi-step strategies and that search (MCTS) is the needed next axis.
- **cassiusfive/balatro-gym** (Python Gymnasium env, targets v1.0.0), **evanofslack/balatro-rs** (Rust engine + move generator with a byte-accurate seed/RNG sub-crate, MIT), **CzJLee/Balatro-AI**, **raienelliston/Balatro-Bot** (OpenGL+Tesseract vision).
- **FFFishes7/blinddeck** — an "agent-oriented" play desk extending BalatroBot with a `glance` state summary, a wiki-backed `know` lookup, and one-action-per-turn helpers, aimed at Cursor/Codex/Claude agents. A `balatro-codex-mcp` MCP server also exists.
- Seed tooling: **SpectralPack/Immolate** (OpenCL seed searcher replicating Balatro's exact RNG), **OptimusPi/Ouija** (MIT, based on Immolate; successor: BalatroSeedOracle), plus web analyzers (balatrohq, balatrocalc, balatroseeds.com).

**Gap assessment vs. your goal.** The existing BalatroBench (a) reports a single aggregate ("rounds reached") that conflates all three horizons, (b) runs only the easiest config (Red/White), (c) has no contamination controls, (d) uses a small fixed public seed set (invites overfitting/memorization), and (e) is really a tool-calling reliability harness as much as a strategy benchmark. None of these directly measure "how well does the model balance immediate vs. short vs. long horizons," which is your actual thesis.

### 2. The game-benchmark landscape and why it's inadequate for your goal
- **BALROG** (Paglieri et al., ICLR 2025; arXiv 2411.13543): six RL game environments (BabyAI, Crafter, TextWorld, MiniHack, NetHack, Baba Is AI), text + vision, 0–100 progression scoring. Key finding on long horizons: per Table 14, o1-preview scored **1.57 ± 0.40 % average progress** on the NetHack Learning Environment — the highest of any tested model, which the paper text calls "a meager 1.5% average game progression." Vision observations often *hurt*. Long-horizon planning is the documented weakness — exactly your target capability.
- **lmgame-Bench** (Hu et al., ICLR 2026; arXiv 2505.15146): Super Mario, Sokoban, Tetris, 2048, Candy Crush, Ace Attorney via a unified Gym API with modular perception/memory/reasoning scaffolds; explicitly targets the three biggest problems — brittle perception, prompt sensitivity, and data contamination. The most directly relevant methodology to copy.
- **Orak** (Park et al., ICLR 2026; arXiv 2506.03610): 12 games across genres via MCP, plus fine-tuning trajectories; observation-to-text via OCR/memory; tests zero-shot/reflection/planning agent modules.
- **VideoGameBench** (Zhang et al.; arXiv 2505.18134): 10–20 1990s games from raw pixels, minimal scaffolding. Per the abstract, "the best performing models, Gemini 2.5 Pro and Claude 3.7 Sonnet, complete only 0.48% of VideoGameBench and 1.6% of VideoGameBench Lite" (Lite pauses the game for the model). Shows pure-vision real-time play is currently near-hopeless.
- **Factorio Learning Environment** (Hopkins et al., NeurIPS 2025; arXiv 2503.09617): open-ended automation; code-as-action; agents show short-horizon skill but fail complex multi-step automation and spatial reasoning. A good "non-saturating" design template.
- **SmartPlay, TextArena, GameBench, GVGAI-LLM, KORGym, OmniGameArena** — additional entrants; SmartPlay and GameBench are the earlier multi-game agent suites.
- **Pokémon efforts** (Claude Plays Pokémon; Gemini Plays Pokémon): the central cautionary tale. The GPP developer explicitly warned it is **not** a clean benchmark because Claude and Gemini used different harnesses — "please don't consider this a benchmark… you can't really make direct comparisons." Noam Brown made the same point publicly ("would love to see a Pokémon eval with a standardized harness, or no harness at all"). Recent work (Continual Harness, arXiv 2605.09998) shows harnesses can even rewrite themselves mid-run. The lesson: **scaffolding/harness effects can dominate the model signal**, so a credible benchmark needs a single frozen harness.
- **ARC-AGI** as a contrast: abstract, contamination-resistant reasoning with no memorizable walkthroughs — the property you want to import into a game setting.

The literature consensus (BALROG, FLE, plus the 2025–2026 long-horizon-agent RL papers on credit assignment): current LLMs have promising short-horizon skills but weak long-horizon planning, weak credit assignment across many decisions, and error accumulation over long trajectories. Those are exactly the capabilities Balatro stresses.

### 3. Technical routes to hook an LLM to Balatro
**Route A — BalatroBot mod (recommended MVP).** Balatro is Lua on LÖVE (Love2D). The community modding stack is **Lovely** (runtime Lua injector) + **Steamodded** (mod loader). BalatroBot is a Steamodded mod that:
- serves **JSON-RPC 2.0 over HTTP** (default 127.0.0.1:12346);
- extracts a clean JSON state from the global `G` object (game info, hand, jokers, consumables, shop), filtering UI/circular refs, and only returns state when the event queue is stable (`#G.E_MANAGER.queues.base < EVENT_QUEUE_THRESHOLD`, i.e. <3 events);
- supports actions for card select, play/discard, shop buy/sell/reroll, blind select/skip, consumable use, state management;
- has a Python package/CLI with `--fast`, `--debug`, `--headless`, and `--render-on-api` flags, cross-platform launchers (macOS/Windows/Linux-Proton/native Love2D), and integration tests that spin up instances on random ports (→ parallelism);
- ships an OpenRPC spec (`openrpc.json`).

**Determinism/seeding.** Balatro uses seeded PRNG: entering a seed string fully determines shops, packs, bosses, tags, vouchers and most joker/card RNG; separate RNG streams per element keep seeded runs stable (a Lucky Card trigger doesn't change the next shop). Identical seed + identical actions → identical outcomes. The seed space is large: the community Balatro Seed Tracker (balatroseeds.com) states "There are 2,318,107,019,760 possible seeds in Balatro!"; the Balatro Wiki notes any character except O and 0 can appear, giving 34⁸ ≈ 1.8 trillion generatable seeds. Either way, ample room for held-out seed sets.

**Speed/headless.** In-game speed maxes at 4×; community mods push far beyond (balatro-ultimate-speedup, Tukajo/balatro_speed_mod, MoreSpeed to 16×, Nopeus). BalatroBot's `--fast`/`--headless` plus a speed mod get you fast, animation-skipped, server-side runs. taggarttufte's project confirms live-game training is throughput-limited (RAM degradation at ~8 parallel Lua instances, ~14 steps/s), which is why they pivoted to a pure-Python sim (~1000+ steps/s).

**Route B — Open-source simulators (for scale/speed and contamination variants).**
- **taggarttufte/balatro_sim** (Python): full ruleset, 164 jokers, 12 planets/22 tarots/18 spectrals/27 vouchers, 15 boss effects, 496 passing tests. Not a standalone package but the most complete open Python reimplementation.
- **cassiusfive/balatro-gym** (Python Gymnasium, targets v1.0.0). Auto-generated docs claim "all 150+ jokers, boss blinds, consumables" and a 16-stream deterministic RNG; the README itself is sparse — verify coverage and the LICENSE file directly.
- **evanofslack/balatro-rs** (Rust, MIT): rules engine + exhaustive move generator + Python bindings (`pylatro`); explicitly a *simplified subset* (no tarots/spectrals/boss modifiers/stakes/decks) — BUT its `balatro-seed` sub-crate is a **byte-accurate port of Balatro's seed/RNG**.

Simulators give you 10,000×+ throughput and freedom to inject custom rules, at the cost of fidelity drift from the real game.

**Route C — Vision/pixels** (proj-airi, raienelliston). Higher cost, brittle perception, but tests the perception+reasoning stack end-to-end. VideoGameBench shows pure vision is currently near-hopeless for full runs. Recommendation: **text-state first**, offer an optional vision track later.

**IP / legal / community.** Balatro is commercial closed-source (LocalThunk/Playstack); however the LÖVE executable appends a readable Lua archive, so the community routinely decompiles it (7-Zip on Balatro.exe) — this is how modding works. LocalThunk keeps a deliberate distance from modding ("recognises that the modding community flourishes but keeps his distance as to not affect his creative input"); there is no official mod API and he has said a native mod interface is unlikely. **Critically, LocalThunk is publicly and strongly anti-AI-"art"**: after a subreddit moderator allowed labelled AI art, he intervened, banned AI-generated content from r/balatro, and said (on Bluesky/Reddit) that neither he nor Playstack condone AI "art" and that "it does real harm to artists of all kinds." That stance is specifically about generative *art*, not about AI *playing* the game, but it means: (a) an official endorsement is unlikely; (b) branding/PR should be handled carefully and respectfully; (c) do not use any AI-generated Balatro art in the project. Running the real game requires each evaluator to own a Steam copy (~$15). You almost certainly **can** legally distribute your own logs/seeds/JSON state and code (seeds are short strings; state is factual game data), but you should not redistribute the game's copyrighted assets/Lua. Existing RL repos include disclaimers ("unofficial… research and educational purposes only") — adopt the same.

### 4. Why Balatro is a good (and partly bad) horizon-balancing testbed
**Structure (verified against balatrowiki.org / balatrogame.fandom.com):**
- 8 antes × 3 blinds (Small 1× / Big 1.5× / Boss ~2× base) = 24 rounds; Boss always played, Small/Big skippable for **Tags**.
- **Base (White) score requirements scale roughly exponentially.** Confirmed via the Wiki's `Module:Blind_Score` base values (Small Blind = base chips): ante 1 = 300, ante 2 = 800, ante 3 = 2,000, ante 4 = 5,000, ante 5 = 11,000, ante 6 = 20,000, ante 7 = 35,000, ante 8 = 50,000 (the Ante 8 boss target on White is ~100,000). Scoring is chips × mult, which itself grows multiplicatively via hand levels/jokers — so the player's engine must grow exponentially to keep pace.
- **Economy**: per the Wiki (balatrowiki.org/w/Interest), "$1 of interest is earned for every $5 the player has at the end of the round, with a cap of $5 each round (which means that any money over $25 won't generate interest)." Seed Money and Money Tree raise the cap "to $10 and $20 respectively (any money over $50 and $100 won't generate interest)." This creates the core tempo-vs-economy tension.
- **8 cumulative stakes**: White (base) → Red (no small-blind reward) → Green (faster scaling) → Black (Eternal stickers, can't sell/destroy) → Blue (−1 discard) → Purple (faster scaling) → Orange (Perishable, debuffed after 5 rounds) → Gold (Rental, $3/round, $1 to acquire). Higher stakes add hard constraints that punish myopic play.
- **Content**: 150 jokers (incl. 5 secret Legendaries via The Soul spectral), 12 planets, 22 tarots, 18 spectrals, 32 vouchers, 15 standard decks (+20 challenge decks).
- **Endless mode** from ante 9; per balatrowiki.org/w/Blinds_and_Antes, "In Ante 39, the score requirement exceeds 2^1024 (1.8e308)… causing the score to overflow to NaN… Ante 39 is the highest Ante reachable in Endless mode" (LocalThunk has called this overflow an "accident" he considers iconic and won't fix).

**Horizon mapping:**
- *Immediate*: which ≤5 of 8 cards to play; which to discard (218 non-empty subsets of 8 cards). Scoreable against a solver — a clean immediate-horizon sub-metric.
- *Short-term*: beat this blind within limited hands/discards; whether to skip a blind for a tag; this ante's shop spend and reroll timing; when to bank for interest.
- *Long-term*: commit to a scaling archetype (flush/pair/high-card/face/etc.), economy-vs-tempo, planning around boss blinds, preparing an engine that clears ante 8+, and stake-specific constraints (eternal/perishable/rental change which jokers are worth buying at all).

**Strategic depth / variance.** Community consensus: White stake is ~99% winnable for strong players; difficulty ramps sharply through the stakes; Gold stake is brutal and "Completionist++" (a gold sticker on all 150 jokers — each joker must be present at the end of a winning Gold Stake run) is near-impossible and took top players 160–265 hours. Skilled humans win ~70% of full runs; random play <0.01%; best public RL ~2.35%. Variance is real (bad boss/blind combos, dead shops), so **many runs are needed** to separate agents — this is a feature (tests consistency) and a cost driver.

**Contamination.** The wiki, joker lists, and strategy guides are all in training data; LLMs will know joker effects and standard heuristics (a Hacker News commenter noted models could even generalize card-game strategy from Google Books). This *helps* rote knowledge but does **not** solve in-run credit assignment, sequencing, or adapting to a specific seed's shop path. Still, to truly test reasoning over memorization you need contamination-robust variants (below).

### 5. Benchmark design considerations (from the agent-benchmark literature)
- **Metrics (horizon-decomposed — your differentiator):**
  - *Immediate sub-score*: per-decision hand-selection optimality vs. an exact/greedy solver (the sim can compute best achievable score for the current hand), averaged over all play/discard decisions.
  - *Short-term sub-score*: blind clear rate given resources; shop-decision quality (did purchases/rerolls improve expected clear probability?); economy efficiency (interest captured vs. spent).
  - *Long-term sub-score*: highest ante reached; win rate per stake; build coherence (do purchases converge on a viable scaling engine?); "engine growth" (score headroom vs. requirement curve over time).
  - *Overall*: win rate per stake, ante-reached distribution, chip-score distributions, plus cost/tokens per run.
- **Reproducibility**: fixed + held-out seed sets; pin game version + mod version + sim version; log full trajectories (state, legal actions, chosen action, model rationale). Report per-seed and aggregate with confidence intervals.
- **Harness design (avoid the Pokémon trap)**: one frozen, shared harness for all models; structured JSON observation as default (natural-language and screenshot tracks optional); strict action validation (BalatroBot already separates valid-executable / valid-non-executable / invalid tool calls); decide explicitly on scratchpad/memory across turns and on tool access (a hand-score calculator is the biggest judgment call — allowing it isolates strategy from arithmetic; disallowing it tests numeric reasoning too; run both as separate tracks). Manage context over hundreds of decisions with a standardized summarizer identical across models.
- **Baselines**: random; greedy heuristic (highest-scoring hand each turn); a strong scripted policy (e.g., balatro_sim heuristics); the existing PPO RL agent (~2.35%); human players at several skill tiers.
- **Statistical rigor**: because roguelike variance is high, use enough seeds/runs (dozens+ per stake) for tight CIs; report per-seed variance; note the cost implications for frontier models (long runs × many seeds × reasoning tokens is expensive — BalatroBench already reports per-tool-call cost, which you should keep).
- **Contamination/robustness variants (the core of your value-add)**: (a) **custom jokers** with novel effects (Joker Forge/Steamodded make this easy) so memorized joker knowledge doesn't transfer; (b) **renamed/reskinned** vanilla jokers (same effect, new name) to test whether models reason from described effects vs. recalled names; (c) **rule perturbations** (altered interest cap, altered scaling, modified boss effects); (d) **procedurally generated variants**; (e) **held-out private seeds** rotated over time (LiveBench-style) to prevent leaderboard overfitting.

### 6. Proposed starting plan (MVP → robust → leaderboard)

**Phase 0 — Decide positioning (1 week).** Because "BalatroBench" is taken and shallow, either (a) **contribute horizon-decomposed metrics + contamination variants upstream** to coder/balatrobench, or (b) **launch a distinct name** (e.g., "Balatro Horizon Bench" / "BHB") that explicitly brands itself as the *reasoning-depth, contamination-robust* benchmark. Recommendation: **(b)**, reusing their open-source BalatroBot/BalatroLLM plumbing (all MIT) so you don't rebuild the mod.

**Phase 1 — MVP on the real game (2–4 weeks).**
- **Route**: BalatroBot mod + a thin Python harness (fork balatrollm or the blinddeck patterns). Real game guarantees fidelity.
- **Observation schema (JSON)**: phase; ante/blind + chip requirement + current score + hands/discards left; full hand (rank/suit/enhancement/edition/seal/debuff); jokers (name, effect text, stickers, sell value); consumables; shop (items, prices, reroll cost); dollars; vouchers; boss effect. Include a `legal_actions` list every turn.
- **Action schema**: `play_hand(indices)`, `discard(indices)`, `buy(shop_idx)`, `sell_joker(idx)`, `reroll`, `use_consumable(idx, targets)`, `select_blind`, `skip_blind`, `leave_shop`. Validate server-side; log the three tool-call validity classes.
- **Config**: start Red deck / White stake (comparable to existing BalatroBench) but immediately add **White→Gold stake sweeps** to get horizon pressure.
- **Scoring v1**: overall (ante reached, win rate/stake) + the three horizon sub-scores, using balatro_sim (or the evanofslack move generator) offline to compute per-hand optimality for the immediate sub-score.
- **Seeds**: 20–30 public seeds for the open leaderboard + a private held-out set for the "official" number.
- **Baselines**: random, greedy, scripted, PPO agent, ≥1 human tier.

**Phase 2 — Contamination-robust variants (4–8 weeks).** Add custom-joker packs, renamed-joker mode, and 2–3 rule perturbations as separate tracks; rotate held-out seeds periodically (LiveBench-style). Report "vanilla vs. perturbed" deltas as a **memorization-vs-reasoning** signal — this is the headline result that distinguishes your benchmark from the existing one.

**Phase 3 — Scale, leaderboard, paper (ongoing).** Move bulk evaluation to a high-fidelity simulator for cost/throughput (validate sim-vs-real agreement on a seed subset first, given taggarttufte's finding that ~30% of jokers were initially mis-implemented), publish a public leaderboard + trajectories dataset, and write it up with the horizon decomposition and contamination-robustness analysis as the contributions.

**Biggest open decisions:**
1. **Real game vs. simulator** for the official number (fidelity vs. cost/throughput; the answer may be "real game for the leaderboard, sim for research variants").
2. **Calculator/tool access** (isolate strategy vs. also test arithmetic — recommend running both tracks).
3. **Memory/scaffold policy** (how much cross-turn memory is "the model" vs "the harness" — freeze it and document it).
4. **Which stakes count** for the headline (Gold stake maximizes horizon pressure but adds variance and cost).

**Main risks:** (1) *Harness dominance* — mitigate with one frozen shared harness (Pokémon lesson). (2) *Cost* — long runs × many seeds × reasoning tokens; budget and report cost. (3) *Variance* — need many seeds; report CIs. (4) *Contamination* — mitigate with the Phase-2 variants. (5) *Legal/community* — require evaluators to own the game, distribute only your code/logs/seeds, add an "unofficial/research" disclaimer, avoid AI-generated art, and keep any public framing respectful given LocalThunk's stance.

## Caveats
- BalatroBench leaderboard specifics (top model gemini-3-pro-preview clearing round 24 nine of 15 times) come from a Feb 2026 GIGAZINE writeup and the live site; the live table renders via JavaScript so I could not extract the full numeric table directly — verify current standings at balatrobench.com.
- The 2.35% RL ceiling and ~70% human win rate are from the taggarttufte/balatro-rl README; the human figure is that author's estimate, and community estimates of White-stake win rates ("~99%") are player opinion, not measured data.
- Some cited arXiv IDs with 2026 dates (credit-assignment/long-horizon papers) and forward-dated model names ("gemini-3-pro-preview," "GPT-6") appear in search results consistent with the stated current date of Sept 14, 2026; treat rapidly-moving leaderboard details as time-sensitive.
- Simulator fidelity: taggarttufte's own audit found ~30% of jokers were initially implemented incorrectly, underscoring that ANY simulator must be validated against the real game before trusting results; balatro-gym's "all 150+ jokers" claim comes from auto-generated docs, not its README; balatro-rs is explicitly a simplified subset (with only its seed/RNG sub-crate byte-accurate).
- Licenses: balatrobench/balatrobot/balatrollm and Ouija are MIT (confirmed); Immolate and balatro-gym licenses should be confirmed from their LICENSE files before redistribution.
- Game-mechanics figures (blind scores, interest cap, ante-39 overflow, content counts) are current as of patch ~1.0.1o-FULL per the Balatro Wiki and could shift with future patches; pin a specific game version for the benchmark.
